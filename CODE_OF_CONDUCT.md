# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**In short:** be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/IoannisZempe/InControl/security/advisories/new) or by direct message to @IoannisZempe. All reports are reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content or ban contributors who violate this code.
